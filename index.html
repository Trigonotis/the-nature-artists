An archive, always becoming

Not apart from nature.
A part of it.

The Nature Artists brings together art that engages with ecological questions: how we use land, what we leave behind, whose lives we notice, and what we choose to care for.

This is a starting point, not a complete survey. We connect each work to its sources and welcome practices, places, and perspectives yet to be represented.
